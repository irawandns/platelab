# Indonesian household plate references

Research date: 12 October 2026. This is a reference brief for discussing plate identity, dimensions, and visual familiarity before deciding features.

## Findings and evidence limits

The useful reference set includes brown-rimmed ceramic, floral ceramic, transparent or tinted glass, melamine, enamel, and ayam-jago ceramic plates, plus compartment meal trays as a special category. Their identities involve material, decoration, rim construction, and profile as well as color.

This research combines Indonesian cultural coverage, manufacturer catalogs, and individual product listings. Listings establish that a specific product exists and provide example dimensions; they do not establish national household ownership. Cultural articles support familiarity and nostalgia, but are not representative surveys. Recognition will vary with region, generation, and family habits. No reliable nationwide ranking of plate types was established.

Several selected examples cluster around 21.5–23.5 cm. This is a useful reference range for these particular familiar plates, not a claim that every Indonesian household uses this size. Larger everyday plates also exist.

For reference matching, preserve the **five-well layout, rounded corners, raised separators, and metallic finish**. Measure length and width separately rather than using a diameter. Record each well's footprint, depth, and separator height from a physical sample or a dimensioned drawing. Overall tray height alone does not establish well depth.

Food assignments can remain flexible: a large well could hold rice, and smaller wells could hold lauk, vegetables, fruit, or sauce. These are possible uses, not fixed assignments visible in the image. Compartment areas also should not be treated as validated nutrition proportions.

## Size and shape observations

- **Nominal inches and centimeters can disagree.** Hoover calls its 22.6 cm plate 9 inches, whereas other sellers use 23 cm for that label. Prefer the published metric dimension of a chosen reference.
- **Outer diameter does not establish the usable food area.** Rim width, the slope of the shoulder, and the flat well diameter determine how a serving sits. The selected sources rarely measure these explicitly.
- **A 3.5–4 cm overall height does not mean a 3.5–4 cm deep food well.** The foot, material thickness, and rim elevation all contribute. Profile measurements need a side view or a real sample.
- **Material thickness and overall height are separate measurements.** Hoover's 2.4 cm measurement is the plate height, not its wall thickness.
- **Size changes the visual impression of a portion.** The outer circular area of a 26 cm plate is approximately 28% greater than that of a 23 cm plate, calculated as `(26 / 23)^2 - 1`. Actual usable area depends on the profile.

## Relationship to the current app

The current skin definitions in `src/lib/plate-skins.ts` provide Cream, Sage, and Blue palettes. The documented model has a 26 cm default diameter, 20/26/32 cm presets, fixed 3.5 cm height, and a flat eating area equal to 70% of outer diameter. See [plate and food physics](plate-and-food-physics.md).

The existing cream/brown appearance is close to the list-coklat family. The researched Lucky example is smaller at 21.5 cm. It should not stand in for glass, enamel, and melamine profiles without checking those specific references.

## Research recommendation before feature planning

Use **list coklat, white floral ceramic, glass, melamine, and enamel** as the first comparison set. Include **ayam jago** as a recognizable cultural reference, while checking whether it evokes home meals or warung meals for the intended audience. Add the user's **five-compartment stainless meal tray** as a special vessel reference. This is a proposed research shortlist, not a popularity ranking or an implementation decision.

For each selected real plate, record outer diameter, overall height, flat well diameter, rim width, a top photograph, and a side photograph. For glass and enamel, also record edge thickness and underside construction. Published specifications currently do not justify exact models for all these details.

To establish household recognition, show the source photographs to Indonesian users from different ages and regions and ask which they have actually used at home, their local name for it, and whether it was used daily or for guests. That would resolve the biggest remaining uncertainty: which references fit this app's users best.
